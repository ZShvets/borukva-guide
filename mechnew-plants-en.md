# 🌾New Plants

>Who are you calling a vegetable?

Cucumbers, tomatoes, and many other new plants that you can read about below.

<PlantsList sectionTitle="Vegetables" />


### Seeds

Each vegetable can be crafted into 3 seeds.

### On Farmland

Usually, planting is done on regular farmland, but there are exceptions, such as:

- **Rice** - grows in water. To plant it, create farmland with a hoe and place a water block above it.

### Crates

Crates are a convenient way to store large quantities of vegetables, or they can be used for decoration. A crate is crafted from 9 vegetables of the same type.

<PlantsList sectionTitle="Berries" />

All berry types are similar to vanilla **Sweet Berries**.

<PlantsList sectionTitle="Trees" />


When a tree grows up, it gets flowering leaves - clicking on these leaves drops new fruits. After a while, they regenerate.
